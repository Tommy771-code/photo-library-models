AuraFace mean face (face_mean_auraface_r100.npy)

What it is: one unit vector of 512 float32 numbers, the average of 325 AuraFace R100 face embeddings. The app uses it for
its vague-face test: a face as alike as this to the average says too little about who it is to start a person. No face,
picture or person can be read back from an average of several hundred faces, and no face, crop, picture or per-face vector
is shipped.

Source: 325 faces of strangers in 188 everyday photos from the COCO 2017 dataset (https://cocodataset.org), originally
published on Flickr, which form part of the app's clean-room test library. Faces were found by RetinaFace-R50 (score 0.85
or more), aligned and embedded by the app's own code. Left out: every photo of a named or public person (the test
library's people photos and the faces checked to be public figures), every photo not under one of the two licences below,
and all of the app owner's photos (none were used at any step).
